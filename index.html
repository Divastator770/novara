About Novara

A learning centre built around how AP exams are actually graded.

Novara Academy started with one idea: most AP prep either moves too fast for the curriculum or too slow for the exam. We split the difference — full syllabus coverage, paired every week with timed, rubric-scored practice.

  • 01

    Small cohorts, real feedback

    Every class stays under 12 students, so every free-response answer gets read and scored, not just marked complete.

  • 02

    Subject specialists, not generalists

    Your Calculus BC instructor teaches Calculus BC. Your Micro instructor has graded AP exams. No one covers all six subjects at once.

  • 03

    Practice under real exam conditions

    Monthly full-length, timed practice exams with the same section structure and scoring style as the real AP exam.
